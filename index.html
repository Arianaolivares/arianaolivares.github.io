About

I am an M.S. student in Mechanical Engineering at Tufts University, advised by Prof. Markus Nemitz in the Nemitz Robotics Group. My research focuses on advanced manufacturing for soft robotics, particularly fabrication methods that reduce the cost and technical barriers of producing soft robotic components.

My thesis evaluates injection molding with a pellet extrusion head as a route to fabricating ultra-soft elastomer components, contributing to the lab’s effort to make soft robots that can be built with widely available 3D printing hardware. Previously, I completed two Stanford Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowships in the Zhao Lab, where I studied magnetically actuated soft metamaterials.

I also serve as a teaching assistant in robotics at Tufts and am committed to broadening access to engineering education.
