About

I am an M.S. student in Mechanical Engineering at Tufts University, advised by Prof. Markus Nemitz in the Nemitz Robotics Group. My research focuses on low-cost fabrication methods for soft robotics, and my thesis evaluates injection molding with a pellet extrusion head for ultra-soft elastomer components.

Previously, I spent two summers as a Stanford Summer Undergraduate Research Fellow in Prof. Renee Zhao’s Soft Intelligent Materials Lab, working on magneto-mechanical metamaterials. I currently TA Senior Capstone Design at Tufts and teach K–8 engineering through the Tufts Center for Engineering Education and Outreach.
